POWERSYNCPRO MIGRATION AGENT

Workstation migration guidance

The PowerSyncPro Migration Agent may display a QR code or clickable link that brings you directly to the relevant section of this page.

Migration stages Cache credentials Migration available Migration starting Migration in progress Migration complete Service unavailable Legal notice
01

Cache credentials

You may be asked to provide credentials for your destination account so PowerSyncPro can prepare your workstation for sign-in after migration.

Only enter credentials into the PowerSyncPro Migration Agent when prompted. If you are not the primary user of this computer, contact your IT support team.
02

Migration available

Your workstation is ready to migrate. Save your work, close applications, connect the device to power and use a reliable network connection before starting.

03

Migration starting

PowerSyncPro is preparing the computer for migration. Leave the computer switched on and do not interrupt the process. A restart may occur automatically.

04

Migration in progress

Your workstation migration is in progress. Do not power off the computer unless instructed by your IT support team. The device may restart more than once.

05

Migration complete

The workstation migration has completed. Sign in using your destination account. Applications and policies may continue to configure after first sign-in.

!

Service unavailable

The migration service is temporarily unavailable or the workstation cannot communicate with PowerSyncPro. Check the network connection and try again. If the message persists, contact IT support.
